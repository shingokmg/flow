    Minimal focus tool

    flow is a minimal Pomodoro timer and Ivy Lee-style task manager for focused work.

    flow is a beautifully constrained focus surface for deciding less and doing the next important thing. No sign-ups, no cloud sync. Everything runs locally in your browser to keep your tasks private and your mind clear.

    Source on GitHub

    Work through the day with fewer decisions

    Write down today’s six most important tasks, put them in order, and work from the top. The interface is built to reduce choice at the moment of action, so you can stay focused on the next important task instead of reorganizing your list all day.

    A quiet 25/5 Pomodoro timer

    flow includes a minimal 25 minute work timer and a 5 minute break timer. The timer stays visible, simple, and readable, helping you keep a steady rhythm without turning your workspace into a dashboard.

    Private by default

    Tasks, timing, and daily logs stay in your browser with local storage. There is no account system, no sync layer, and no remote database. You can use flow instantly and keep your work data on your own device.
