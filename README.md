# flow

flow is a minimal Pomodoro timer and Ivy Lee-style task manager for focused work.

flow is a beautifully constrained focus surface for deciding less and doing the next important thing.

No sign-ups, no cloud sync. Everything runs locally in your browser to keep your tasks private and your mind clear.

## What It Does

- Shows the current time and today's weather in a quiet, always-on clock panel.
- Runs a fixed Pomodoro timer with `Work` and `Break` modes.
- Lets you write down today's six most important tasks, put them in order, and work from the top.
- Stores everything locally in your browser with `localStorage`.

## Design Principles

- Reduce decisions before work starts.
- Keep the next important thing visually obvious.
- Prefer calm constraints over feature-heavy productivity tooling.
